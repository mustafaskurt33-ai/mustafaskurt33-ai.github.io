Privacy Policy

Domain Manager · Effective November 22, 2026

Domain Manager is an independent prototype for organizing information about .pi domains. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pi Network.

Information handled

When you choose to sign in through Pi Browser, the app receives your Pi username through the Pi SDK and displays it in your current session. This prototype does not send the sign-in access token to an application server or store it in local storage. Authentication is not verified by an application backend.

Domain names, categories, descriptions, and optional website links you submit are stored locally in your current browser using browser storage. They are not automatically shared with other users or synchronized between devices.

Storage and retention

Locally saved listings remain in your browser until you remove them within the app or clear your browser's site data. The displayed Pi username is held for the current page session only. Your browser, GitHub Pages (the website host), and Pi Network may process technical information according to their own policies.

Use and sharing

The prototype uses submitted domain information only to display and manage your local list. It does not currently run a shared public directory, sell your information, or offer payments. Pi authentication uses Pi Network's services, subject to their separate terms and privacy practices.

Your choices

You may remove individual domain listings using the Delete option, clear local browser data, or choose not to use Pi Sign-In. Avoid submitting sensitive personal information in listing descriptions.

Changes and contact

Features and this policy may change as the prototype develops. Material changes should be reflected in an updated policy. For questions, please open an issue in the project's GitHub repository.
